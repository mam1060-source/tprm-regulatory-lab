Interagency guidance for banks

Final guidance. The Federal Reserve, FDIC, and OCC issued joint guidance in June 2023. It covers planning, due diligence and selection, contract negotiation, ongoing monitoring, and termination. Governance, independent review, and documentation support the full lifecycle.

September 2026 proposal. The agencies, joined by NCUA, proposed a replacement that emphasizes tailoring oversight to the risk of each relationship. The proposal is open for comment through December 16, 2026. The 2023 guidance remains the final interagency guidance for banks as of November 16, 2026.
