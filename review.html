Apply the guidance to this vendor

Use the review plan above as a starting point for a risk assessment. The examples below show how the 2023 interagency guidance can inform decisions across the relationship.

Planning
Identify the business owner and assess the impact if the vendor mishandles customer data, misroutes a case, or becomes unavailable.
Due diligence and selection
Review evidence of data handling, access controls, service continuity, and the vendor’s testing of routing accuracy. Adjust the depth of review to the activity’s risk.
Contract negotiation
Consider terms for data use and deletion, incident notification, access to records, subcontractors, and transition support.
Ongoing monitoring
Assign an owner to track routing errors, incidents, and material changes. Set a review schedule and escalation triggers based on the assessed risk.
Termination
Plan how staff would resume routing, retrieve records, revoke access, and confirm data return or deletion.
